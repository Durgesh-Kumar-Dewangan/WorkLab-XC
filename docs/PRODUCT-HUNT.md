## Description (long)

WorkLab XC turns the syllabus you already have into a complete way of studying. Paste or upload it, and the app reads it into units and topics, writes a lesson for every topic, and teaches each one in a 3D classroom: a lecture-hall screen that changes its look for the subject (physics, chemistry, biology, maths, computer science, history, language, business, arts, health), real-world photographs for examples, spoken narration with your device's own voice, captions, and camera views you can switch between.

You choose how deep a lesson goes. Quick gives a short overview. Standard is the usual lesson. Deep starts from zero and builds the idea one step at a time, with plain-language definitions, the common mistakes, and diagrams drawn on the page so they stay sharp on every screen. Practice questions, rounds and exams are written from what the lesson actually taught, so you are tested on what you just learned.

Around the lessons sit the habits that make people come back: a learning path that unlocks one topic at a time (six visual styles to pick from), Duolingo-style practice rounds where right answers in a row double your points, a daily goal and streak, gems and hearts, and a weekly and all-time leaderboard you can filter by learner type and syllabus. A study coach adds an error map, spaced-repetition cards, an exam planner, past papers, teach-it-back and viva practice. A canvas lets you ask a question and branch it into a visual board of answers, diagrams and pictures.

It is built to be cheap to run and fair to price. A lesson, picture or deck written once for a topic is stored and shared, so the next learner who asks for the same topic gets it instantly with no new AI cost. Prices follow where you live: rupees in India, US dollars elsewhere in three levels, with Razorpay and PayPal, receipts for every payment, and plans that renew automatically only if you choose.

Your privacy is built in: you can switch off sharing of what you generate, export or delete your data, and developers can only see how an account is doing, never what a learner made or said, with every look recorded in a log the learner can read.

## Who it is for
Students (school, college, competitive exams), self-learners, and teachers who want a ready-made lesson, practice and exam set for any syllabus.

## FAQ for the comments
- **Is it free?** Yes: unlimited learning sessions for one subject at a time, with ads.
- **Which devices?** Any modern browser; the 3D classroom has a lighter mode for older devices.
- **Which languages?** Lessons are written in English today.
- **How do payments work?** Razorpay in India (UPI, cards, netbanking) and PayPal or cards elsewhere. Auto-renew is optional and every payment gets a receipt.
- **Is my data private?** You can stop sharing what you generate, export everything, or delete your account. Support can only see account status, never your content.
